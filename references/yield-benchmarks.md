# Airbtics Yield Benchmarks & Sector Strategy for Casablanca

Data benchmarks collected from **Airbtics** short-term rental analytics for Casablanca and surrounding coastal areas:

## 1. City-Wide Baseline
* **City Average Annual Revenue**: ~$12,400 / unit (~125,000 MAD)
* **City Average Occupancy Rate**: 52% - 58%
* **City Average Daily Rate (ADR)**: 550 MAD - 750 MAD

---

## 2. Comprehensive Priority Matrix

### Tier 1: Ultra High Yield (`[P1]`)
* **`[P1-1]` Coastline & Beachfront Resorts (`+51%`)**
  * *Sectors*: La Corniche, Aïn Diab, Boulevard de l'Océan, Route d'Azemmour, Tamaris, Sidi Rahal, Blanca Beach, El Mansouria, Sidi Bouzid.
  * *ADR*: 1,200 MAD - 2,500 MAD (summer peaks up to 3,500 MAD for seaside chalets/villas).
  * *Target Guest*: International vacationers, diaspora, luxury staycations.
* **`[P1-2]` Anfa (`+21%`)**
  * *Sectors*: Anfa Supérieur, Boulevard d'Anfa.
  * *ADR*: 900 MAD - 1,400 MAD.
  * *Target Guest*: High-net-worth business travelers, diplomats, expatriates.
* **`[P1-3]` Marina & Casa-Port (`+11%`)**
  * *Sectors*: Casablanca Marina, Boulevard des Almohades.
  * *ADR*: 850 MAD - 1,200 MAD.
  * *Target Guest*: Cruise ship tourists, port executives, modern luxury seekers.
* **`[P1-4]` Maârif & Princesses (`+7%`)**
  * *Sectors*: Maârif, Maârif Extension, Les Princesses, Val Fleuri.
  * *ADR*: 650 MAD - 950 MAD.
  * *Occupancy*: Highest year-round occupancy (>70%).
  * *Target Guest*: Shopping tourists, medical tourists, local business travelers.
* **`[P1-5]` Racine (`+0%`)**
  * *Sectors*: Racine, Triangle d'Or.
  * *ADR*: 750 MAD - 1,100 MAD.

---

### Tier 2: High Yield Business & Heritage (`[P2]`)
* **`[P2-6]` Gauthier (`-3%`)**: Fine dining, art galleries, nightlife. High ADR (700-1,000 MAD) with weekend spikes.
* **`[P2-7]` Palmier (`-5%`)**: Business / corporate offices.
* **`[P2-8]` Habous (`-6%`)**: Heritage & artisan district.
* **`[P2-9]` CIL (`-9%`)**: Quiet residential villas.
* **`[P2-10]` Belvédère (`-10%`)**: Direct proximity to Casa-Voyageurs TGV station (quick 1-2 night transit stays).

---

### Tier 3: Core Residential & Corporate (`[P3]`)
* **`[P3-11]` Bourgogne (`-11%`)**: Strong rental volume, affordable coastal proximity.
* **`[P3-12]` Casablanca Finance City - CFC (`-14%`)**: Corporate executives, modern amenities, long-stay discounts.
* **`[P3-13]` Oasis (`-14%`)**: High-end suburban villas and clinics.
* **`[P3-14]` Beauséjour (`-14%`)**: Mid-tier residential hub.
* **`[P3-15]` Centre Ville & Roches Noires (`-19%`)**: Historic center, Derb Omar commercial traders, budget tourism.
* **`[P3-16]` Sidi Maarouf & Nearshore (`-23%`)**: Tech consultants, call center management, offshore IT corporate stays.

---

### Tier 4: Peripheral Residential (`[P4]`)
* **`[P4]` Residential Sectors**:
  * Californie, 2 Mars, Aïn Sebaâ, Oulfa, Hay Hassani, Aïn Chock, Al Fida, Sidi Bernoussi.
  * *Characteristics*: Primarily long-term rental demand; short-term rental yields are lower unless the property features exceptional amenities (pool, private garden).
